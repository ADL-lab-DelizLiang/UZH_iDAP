Advanced Analysis

The final block introduces higher-order, multivariate methods for complex chemical datasets. You will meet principal component analysis and other multivariate techniques, design of experiments (DoE) for exploring many variables efficiently, and a first look at machine learning. These sessions are gateways: each points toward a broader field you can pursue further beyond this course.
